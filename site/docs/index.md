# Mars Ledger

Mars Ledger is a table companion for the Terraforming Mars board game. Each player keeps their player board on
their phone: resources, production, cards, tags and actions. On the TV you see the planet, the players, the
standings and short moments when something big happens.

![The TV in a full game: the 3D board, the global parameters and the players](assets/images/tv-board-3d.png)

    You need a copy of the board game to play.

## Two ways to play

- **Companion.** You play on the physical board. Each player's resources and cards are kept on their phone, and
  the TV shows the game.
- **Full game.** The board is on the TV. Cards and resources are on the phones. The rules are those of the
  open-source Terraforming Mars engine, with bots for empty seats.

## Highlights

- Card lists that show what each card does, and hints with a card's tags, requirements and points in the current
  game.
- Turn planning on your phone: see a move's effect before you confirm it, plan your second action, and pin the
  cards you intend to play next.
- Card moments on the TV: the player's chime, the card held for reading, then its effects one at a time.
- Replay a move on the TV from your phone, and feel a hit on an Android phone as the TV shows it.
