# Playing on phones

Each player uses their own phone. Open the address from the TV's QR code; no app install is needed.

## Joining

1. Scan the QR code on the TV.
2. Pick your profile under **Who are you?**, or tap **Someone new** to make one (name, colour, optional portrait).
   With **Join without a profile**, you play one game and nothing is kept.
3. In the lobby, any seated player can choose the mode, the map, Prelude and the other table settings, add bots,
   and start the game.

Profiles hold your games, wins and achievements across game nights. The last player on each phone is remembered.

## In a full game

Your phone has four tabs: **Hand**, **Table**, **Mars** and **Log**.

<img class="shot-phone" src="../assets/images/phone-hand.png" alt="The Hand tab: resources, production and the cards in hand">
<img class="shot-phone" src="../assets/images/phone-table.png" alt="The Table tab: cards in play, tags and actions">
<img class="shot-phone" src="../assets/images/phone-decision.png" alt="A decision sheet: choosing what to do on your turn">

- **Hand**: your resources and production at the top, then your cards. You can sort and filter the hand.
- **Table**: your cards in play, tags and blue-card actions. Tap the chip under the tabs (**You**, or a
  player's name) to open any player's table, with their tags in one strip.
- **Mars**: the global parameters and the map.
- **Log**: the game as moves. Tap a move to see what it caused; tap a card name to see the card.

On your turn, you get a decision sheet with what the rules allow. If a choice needs a tile, tap a space on
the map.

## Card lists and card hints

<img class="shot-phone" src="../assets/images/phone-play-list.png" alt="Playing a card: each row shows what the card does">
<img class="shot-phone" src="../assets/images/phone-card-hints.png" alt="A card held up, with hints from the current game">

Every list where you pick a card (play a card, sell patents, card actions) shows what each card does under its
name, in up to two lines. A blue card shows its action or effect first. The requirement and fixed victory points
sit in front as small chips. Tap the info button on a row to see the whole card without picking it; swipe to
move through the list.

A held-up card has hints under it with counts from the current game:

- tags: "You have 3 space tags, 4 with this card: +4 M€ production";
- requirements with the current value: "Oxygen 5% max: now 3%";
- other players' tags, cities, greeneries, oceans and adjacent tiles, where they count for the card;
- resources on a card in play and their victory points now;
- the cost of an action against what you have, and discounts against your hand.

The counts follow the rules. Wild tags count for your own effects and minimum requirements, but not for
victory points, maximum requirements or other players' cards. Events in play have no tags. On another player's
table, everything is counted from that player's side. In a companion game you see the same hints in the play step.

## Planning your turn

<img class="shot-phone" src="../assets/images/phone-effect-preview.png" alt="The effect preview while paying for a card">
<img class="shot-phone" src="../assets/images/phone-planner.png" alt="Planning a second action after the first">

Planning stays on your phone. Nobody else sees it, and nothing in the game changes until you make the move.

- **Effect preview.** Before you confirm, you see what the move will do: in the payment step (updated as you adjust
  the payment), when you pick a card action, when you choose a space, and in the heat and plants rows
  of the turn menu. Where the outcome depends on a later choice, you see a range, or "at least", with the reason.
  Drawn cards are a count; their names are never guessed.
- **Plan my second action.** In your first move's sheet, tap **Plan my second action**. You see your numbers after
  the first move, with anything not known yet named, and the moves you could make then. The plan has a dashed
  outline and the label **Planned**.
- **Your planned move.** After your first action, your planned move is at the top of the turn menu, checked
  against the real game: for example "Now 3 M€ short" or "Oxygen is now 6%, above this card's 5% maximum."
  Tap **Confirm** to answer that move's usual questions, **Change** to pick another, or **Drop** to discard it.
  A plan is dropped when you undo, go back, end your turn, or a new generation starts.
- **Plan for next turn.** While others play, tap the chip on the Hand tab to open a tray. Pin the cards you
  intend to play to see them totalled against your M€ now plus next generation's income, with steel and titanium where
  they pay. The chip then reads **Intended**, with the number of cards and their cost.

Plans are kept on this phone for this game and seat, even across a reload.

## Your phone and the TV

With a TV at the table, you can point things out on it from your phone:

- Tap **Replay on the TV** under a move in the **Log** tab to replay it on the TV.
- Tap **Show on the TV** to light up that move's spaces and trackers on the TV's board.
- Tap **Show last move on the TV** to replay the newest move. It is in the game menu and at
  the end of the waiting row as a small button.
- **On the TV now** appears as a small pill while the TV shows a card you just played; your phone stays usable.

The button itself shows the answer: sent, or why not (a replay already on its way, a short wait between replays, or no TV at the table).

When another player hits you (takes resources or lowers your production), you feel it on an Android phone as the
hit lands on the TV. Without a TV, you feel it with the notice. iPhones have no vibration for web pages.

<img class="shot-phone" src="../assets/images/phone-notices.png" alt="Notices: what other players did to you">
<img class="shot-phone" src="../assets/images/phone-log.png" alt="The Log tab, grouped into moves, with Show on the TV">

**Notices** list what happened to you since you last looked: someone took your plants, lowered your
production, or you gained resources from an effect. Attacks stay until you clear them.

## Undo

- While you answer the follow-up questions of your own move, tap **Choose something else** to take the move back.
- After your action, tap **Undo** in the turn menu to take it back, as long as nobody else has moved. If only
  bots moved since, their moves are taken back too.

## In a companion game

You play on the physical board. Your resources and cards are kept on your phone.

- To play a card, type its printed number, search its name, or take a photo of it (when
  [photo scanning](optional-ai.md#photo-scanning) is set up).
- Every change is shown before you confirm it.
- At the end, tap **End the game and score it** in the game menu to move everyone to final scoring.

## The game menu

The menu (the ⋯ button) holds your profile, switching players on a shared phone, the TV radio remote, smart
hints, mission control, the turn clock, bot speed, the poster illustration, and ending or abandoning the game.
Any player can change the table-wide settings.

When **Fly over Mars** is on in the TV options, tap **Fly the TV camera** in the menu to fly with your phone: one
stick to move, one to look, and two buttons for height. The last player to start flies; the others see who is
flying and can take over.

## Reactions and nudges

Tap a sticker to float it across the TV. When another player has been deciding for a while, you can nudge them
with a buzz on their phone.
